About Us

Learn more about EAV Kivumu Technical Secondary School

EAV Kivumu is a Technical Secondary School that helps students gain practical skills so they can work or start businesses after finishing Level 5 (Senior 6). We have dedicated teachers and well-equipped workshops for hands-on practice.

Our Technical Trades

Advanced options taught in Level 4, 5, and 6

SOD
Software Development
Learning to write code, create websites, and mobile apps for the digital world.
BDC
Building Construction
Learning how to build houses, read architectural plans, and use construction materials.
PWO
Public work & Water Option
Learning how to install water pipes, drainage systems, and fix sanitation issues.
AGR
Agriculture
Learning modern farming techniques, growing crops, and soil management practices.
ANH
Animal Health
Learning how to treat sick animals like cows, pigs, and chickens with proper veterinary care.
ACC
Accounting
Learning how to keep financial records for businesses, banks, and organizations.

Ordinary Levels

General subjects before students choose a technical trade

Senior 1 (S1)
Starting secondary school — learning basic sciences, mathematics, and languages to build a strong foundation.
Senior 2 (S2)
Continuing general studies — deepening knowledge in sciences and humanities to prepare for technical pathways.
Senior 3 (S3)
The final year of ordinary level — completing national exams before advancing to TVET trades specialization.
